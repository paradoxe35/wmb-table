# Viewing History

<p class="lead">History keeps a simple record of what you have opened so you can quickly return to recent readings.</p>

## Opening history

In the left menu, click `History`.

The application groups items by day.

  file="en-history-page.png"

## Opening an item from history

Click the item you want and the app will reopen it.

## When history is useful

History helps when:

- you forgot the exact title
- you want to continue a recent study
- you opened many sermons in one day
