# Questions and Troubleshooting

<p class="lead">This page gathers simple solutions for the most common problems.</p>

## I do not see any sermons

Check:

1. that at least one language is installed
2. that one language is active

## Search finds nothing

Try this:

1. check the content language
2. type at least three characters
3. try a simpler word
4. try another form of the same word

## I cannot hear the audio reading

Check:

1. your computer volume
2. `Settings > Audio Reading`
3. the selected voice or provider
4. that playback is not paused

## Sync is not working

Check:

1. your internet connection
2. that you are signed in with Google
3. that sync is active

If needed, sign in again in `Settings > Backup`.
