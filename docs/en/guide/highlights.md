# Managing Your Highlights

<p class="lead">Highlights let you find all the important passages you marked while reading. You can browse them, search through them, and jump back to the original document.</p>

## What highlights are for

When you read a sermon, the Bible, or a personal document, you can highlight a passage to find it later.

All your highlights are then gathered in one place: the **Highlights** page.

This helps you:

- quickly find an important passage
- review your marked passages without searching through each document
- jump directly to the exact spot in your reading

## Opening the highlights page

In the left menu, click `Highlights`.

The page shows all your highlights, from newest to oldest.

## What you see for each highlight

Each item in the list shows:

- **the color** you chose (a small colored dot)
- **the document title** where the passage is located
- **the document type**: sermon, Bible, or personal document
- **a text snippet** of the highlighted passage
- **your comment**, if you added one

## Searching for a highlight

If you have many highlights, use the search bar at the top of the page.

The search looks through:

- the document title
- the highlighted text
- your comments

Simply type a keyword and the results update automatically.

## Going back to the original document

Click on a highlight in the list.

The app opens the corresponding document directly:

- if it is a sermon, it opens in a tab
- if it is a Bible chapter, it opens in a tab
- if it is a personal document, it opens the same way

This is the fastest way to get back to a passage you had noticed.

## Deleting a highlight

If you no longer need a highlight:

1. find it in the list
2. click the delete button on the right
3. confirm

The passage stays in the document, but the color marking and comment are removed.

## Page navigation

If you have many highlights, the list is divided into pages.

Use the navigation buttons at the bottom of the list to move between pages.

## How to create a highlight

Highlights are created from the reading view, not from this page.

Here are the steps:

1. open a sermon, a Bible chapter, or a personal document
2. select the text you want to mark
3. right-click
4. choose `Highlight`
5. choose a color
6. add a comment if you wish
7. save

The passage will then appear here, on the highlights page.

The full reading guide is in [Using the reading view](/en/guide/document-view).

## Synchronization

If backup is enabled, your highlights are automatically synchronized between your devices.

A highlight created on one device appears on the others after the next sync.

## Tips

- Use different colors to distinguish types of passages (for example, one color for promises, another for examples).
- Add a short comment to remind yourself why you kept that passage.
- Review your highlights regularly when preparing your messages.
