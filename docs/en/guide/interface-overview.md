# Understanding the Interface

<p class="lead">Wmb Table is arranged so you can move quickly from reading to preparing your message.</p>

## General view

The main screen is divided into several areas.

## 1. The top bar

At the top of the window, you will find two main views:

- `Tools`: for search, notes, subjects, Bible, and settings
- `Reading`: for reading the content opened in tabs

## 2. The left menu

The left menu contains the working pages:

- `History`
- `Search`
- `Subjects`
- `Notes`
- `Bible`
- `Settings`

You can collapse this menu if you need more space.

## 3. The center area

The center of the window changes depending on what you are doing:

- in `Reading`, you see a sermon, Bible chapter, or personal document
- in `Tools`, you see the page selected from the left menu

## 4. Tabs

Each open document appears in a tab.

This allows you to keep several readings close at hand:

- a main sermon
- another sermon for comparison
- a Bible chapter
- a personal document

## 5. The right panel

This panel is used to open content quickly.

It contains:

- `Sermons`
- `Bible`
- `Documents`

## 6. Settings

Settings are separated into clear sections:

- `General`
- `Languages`
- `Audio Reading`
- `Backup`
- `Update`

## A simple way to remember it

- on the right, you open what you want to read
- in the center, you read
- on the left, you prepare your work
