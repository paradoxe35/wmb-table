# Audio Reading and Voices

<p class="lead">Wmb Table can read text aloud to help you listen to a sermon, a Bible chapter, or one of your own documents.</p>

## What this feature allows

Depending on your settings, you can:

- read text with a voice
- start from a precise passage
- adjust the speed
- choose how the text is followed while it is being read

## Where to set it

Open:

1. `Settings`
2. `Audio Reading`

## Main things to remember

- some voices come from the computer itself
- other voices may need more setup
- the app can switch voices automatically based on language

## Testing a voice

Choose a voice or service, then click `Test voice`.

## Playback controls

From a sermon, Bible chapter, or personal document, you can often:

- start reading
- pause
- resume
- stop
- start from a specific paragraph
