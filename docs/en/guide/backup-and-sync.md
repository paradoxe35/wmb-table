# Backup and Sync

<p class="lead">This feature helps protect your work. It helps Wmb Table keep your notes, subjects, and personal documents so you can find them again later.</p>

## The simple idea

Backup and sync are mainly there so you do not lose your personal work.

They are useful if:

- you change computers
- you install the app again
- you use more than one device
- you want your notes to stay safe

## What can be kept

This mainly concerns:

- notes
- subjects
- personal documents added inside the app
- some useful settings

## Where to open it

1. Open `Settings`
2. Click `Backup`

## What sync means

The word `sync` simply means:

- the application tries to keep your personal data up to date
- it tries to find what you already saved
- it helps you recover your work more easily later

You do not need to understand the internal system. For the user, the idea is simple:

`I work today, and my work stays available later`

## First setup

To begin:

1. click `Google sign in`
2. sign in with the Google account you want to use
3. accept the prompts shown to you
4. return to the application

After that, backup can begin.

## Which Google account to use

It is better to use one Google account for your Wmb Table work.

That helps avoid:

- mixing your data
- forgetting where your work is connected
- signing in to several accounts

## When sync is active

When sync is active, it means the application is trying to keep your personal data current.

For the user, that mainly means:

- your notes are more likely to be available later
- your subjects stay protected
- your personal documents are better preserved

## Active or paused

You can:

- leave sync active
- pause it if you do not want to use it for a while

If you already started writing notes, it is usually better to leave it active.

## What may happen on a new computer

If you install Wmb Table on another computer and sign in with the same account:

- the app may find your saved data
- it may start restoring it
- you may see progress on screen

This helps you continue your work more easily.

## Restoring your data

During restore, you may see:

- the beginning of recovery
- a progress display
- the end of recovery
- sometimes a request to restart the app

Just let the application finish.

## How to know things are okay

Usually things are fine if you can see:

- your Google account shown
- an `Active` state
- no error message

## If sync is paused

If sync is paused:

1. open `Backup`
2. check the switch
3. turn it back on if you want your work protected

## If an error appears

The most common causes are:

- no internet connection
- expired Google session
- incomplete Google permission

In that case:

1. check your internet connection
2. return to `Backup`
3. sign in again if needed
4. wait a moment and check again

## If you are unsure

If you are not sure whether your work is protected:

1. open `Backup`
2. check that your account is shown
3. confirm that the state is not paused
4. look at your notes and subjects to make sure they are present

## What this does not replace

Even with backup and sync, keep good habits:

- give clear names to your notes
- organize references in subjects
- export an important note to PDF if you want
- check from time to time that backup is still active

## Very simple summary

If you want to remember only the main point:

1. connect your Google account
2. leave sync active
3. check from time to time that everything still looks normal
