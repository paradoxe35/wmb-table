# Backup and Sync

<p class="lead">Backup protects your personal work and helps you recover it later, especially if you change computers or use more than one device.</p>

## What is included

Backup mainly matters for your personal data, such as:

- notes
- subjects
- personal documents
- some useful settings

## Where to open it

Open:

1. `Settings`
2. `Backup`

## First connection

1. Click `Google sign in`.
2. Sign in with the account you want.
3. Allow the requested access.
4. Return to the application.

## Active or paused

You can leave sync active or pause it for a time.

## Restoring data

When you connect on a new installation, the application may restore your saved data and show progress.

## If you see an error

The most common causes are:

- no internet connection
- expired Google session
- missing or refused Google access
