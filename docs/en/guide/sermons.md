# Reading Sermons

<p class="lead">Reading sermons is the heart of Wmb Table. This page shows how to open, follow, and return to the messages you need.</p>

## Opening a sermon

In the right panel, open the `Sermons` section.

You can browse the library:

- by year
- by alphabet
- by the available list in the content language

Click a title to open it in the center.

## Reading in the center

When the sermon opens:

- its title appears in a tab
- the text is shown paragraph by paragraph
- you can read without leaving the window

For a detailed explanation of the top bar, right-click menu, highlighting, adding to a subject, adding to a note, and read aloud, also read [Using the Reading View](/en/guide/document-view).

## Using several tabs

You can keep multiple items open at the same time, for example:

- one main sermon
- one Bible chapter
- another sermon for comparison
- one personal document

## Changing translation

Some sermons may be available in more than one translation or installed language.

This helps when you want to compare wording or review the same message in another language.

## Returning to a precise passage

Wmb Table can bring you back to a precise place from:

- search results
- a subject
- a note reference

## Searching inside the open sermon

Inside the reading view, you can search within the sermon that is already open.

This helps when you already know which sermon you need and only want the exact paragraph.

## Highlights and saved passages

Important passages can be marked so they are easier to find again later.

## Zoom and reading comfort

If the text is too small or too large, use the zoom controls in the reading view.

## Audio and voice reading

Depending on the content and settings:

- some sermons may have linked audio
- otherwise the app can read the text with a configured voice

See [Audio Reading and Voices](/en/guide/audio-and-tts).
